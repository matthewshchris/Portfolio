About Me

Designer first. Developer second. Always creative.

I'm Hunter — a multi-disciplinary creative who started out in digital media and content creation before earning a spot on a professional web development team. I spent two years working alongside experienced engineers, contributing UI/UX design and frontend work across multiple brand properties.

My work doesn't stop at the screen. I design for print, create brand identities, illustrate digitally and traditionally, and produce artwork built for the real world — from roadside signage to storefront glass. I also work fluidly with AI tools to push what's possible, faster.

I thrive in collaborative environments where strong design instincts and solid frontend execution make a real difference.
